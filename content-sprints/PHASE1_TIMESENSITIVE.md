# Phase 1 — Time-Sensitive Keywords

**Opportunity:** 235K/mo searches, 0% coverage  
**Expected traffic:** +800-1K visits/mo ($9.6-12K/yr)  
**Effort:** 15-18 hours  
**Timeline:** 2 weeks

---

## Posts to Create (8 core + 4 expandable)

### **CORE POSTS (High Volume, Quick Wins)**

**1. "Gabarito Oficial ENEM 2026 — Respostas Corrigidas"**
- **Volume:** 45K/mo searches
- **Intent:** Answer-focused, FAQ schema
- **Format:** Accordion (searchable by subject)
- **Structure:** 
  - Opening: When will ENEM 2026 results be released?
  - By Subject: Math, Portuguese, History, Geography, Science
  - FAQ: How to challenge an answer? What if there's an error?
- **Effort:** 2 hours
- **Internal Links:** Link from 50+ posts asking "Como passar no ENEM"

**2. "ENEM 2026 — Cronograma de Inscrição e Datas Importantes"**
- **Volume:** 28K/mo searches
- **Intent:** Timeline, when-focused
- **Format:** Timeline (visual) + table
- **Structure:**
  - Registration open/close dates
  - Exam dates (first and second days)
  - Results date
  - Deadline for institution binding
- **Effort:** 1.5 hours

**3. "ENEM 2026 Adiado? Saiba as Novas Datas | Editais Oficiais"**
- **Volume:** 18K/mo searches
- **Intent:** News/updates
- **Format:** Current status + FAQ
- **Structure:**
  - Current exam date status
  - History of postponements (2020+)
  - What to do if exam is postponed
- **Effort:** 1.5 hours

**4. "Validade da Nota do ENEM 2024/2025 — Quanto Tempo Vale?"**
- **Volume:** 22K/mo searches
- **Intent:** Validity, rules
- **Format:** Comprehensive guide + FAQ
- **Structure:**
  - Score validity: 5 years (explained)
  - Use cases (SISU, ProUni, etc.)
  - Score rechecking process
  - When score expires
- **Effort:** 2 hours

**5. "Como Consultar Resultado do ENEM 2026 — Passo a Passo"**
- **Volume:** 35K/mo searches
- **Intent:** How-to, step-by-step
- **Format:** Tutorial + screenshot guide
- **Structure:**
  - Access gov.br portal
  - Enter CPF + password
  - Download certificate
  - Troubleshooting (forgotten password, CPF issues)
- **Effort:** 1.5 hours

**6. "Desempenho por Competência no ENEM — Entenda Sua Nota"**
- **Volume:** 12K/mo searches
- **Intent:** Explanation, understanding
- **Format:** Guide with examples
- **Structure:**
  - 5 competencies explained (each subject)
  - How they're scored
  - How to improve each
  - Examples of weak vs strong answers
- **Effort:** 2.5 hours

**7. "ENEM 2026 Cancelamento — Reembolso, Regras, Exceções"**
- **Volume:** 8K/mo searches
- **Intent:** Cancellation policy
- **Format:** Policy guide + FAQ
- **Structure:**
  - Cancellation deadline
  - Refund process
  - Medical justification
  - Appeals process
- **Effort:** 1.5 hours

**8. "Aplicativo Oficial do ENEM 2026 — Download e Funcionalidades"**
- **Volume:** 14K/mo searches
- **Intent:** Tool discovery, how-to
- **Format:** Download guide + tutorial
- **Structure:**
  - Download links (iOS/Android)
  - Features overview
  - Troubleshooting (login, app crashes)
- **Effort:** 1.5 hours

### **BONUS POSTS (Expandable, Affiliate + Monetization)**
