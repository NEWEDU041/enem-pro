WHAT EACH SCRIPT DOES
================================================================================

gsc-monitoring.sh (Weekly)
────────────────────────
  1. Creates baseline metrics CSV (URLs indexed, position, CTR, etc.)
  2. Initializes keyword ranking tracker for 20 ENEM keywords
  3. Sets up content performance tracker
  4. Creates backlink opportunity tracker
  5. Defines alert thresholds for automation
  6. Generates weekly report template
  7. Creates detailed checklist for weekly tasks

weekly-seo-optimizer.sh (Weekly)
────────────────────────────────
  1. Scans all blog posts in src/pages/blog/
  2. Checks meta descriptions (120-160 chars target)
  3. Validates internal linking (3+ per post minimum)
  4. Verifies ENEM keyword presence
  5. Checks content length (600+ words target)
  6. Reports statistics & optimization gaps
  7. Generates detailed optimization log
  8. Git auto-commit support
